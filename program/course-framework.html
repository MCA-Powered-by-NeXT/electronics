Standard Course Framework

Required operating principles for every NeXTHands CETa-prep class session.

Framework Principles

PrincipleClassroom MeaningEvidence
Safety FirstEvery class begins with a safety brief and every lab follows approved low-voltage boundaries.Safety brief, PPE check, lab shutdown record.
ProfessionalismStudents communicate clearly, follow procedures, arrive prepared, and respect the lab environment.Instructor observation and professionalism rubric.
AccountabilityTools, PPE, components, meters, and assignments are tracked from check-in to shutdown.Tool inventory and PPE accountability records.
Hands-On LearningStudents build, measure, troubleshoot, and document using approved training systems.Lab worksheets and practical checkoffs.
DocumentationStudents record measurements, observations, repairs, and reflections as technician evidence.Measurement logs, repair tickets, inspection forms.
Workforce ReadinessStudents practice conduct expected in entry-level technician work.Career readiness activities and instructor coaching.
Continuous ImprovementStudents and instructor complete AARs and use feedback to improve the next class.AAR log and corrective actions.
ETA CETa PreparationLessons, labs, quizzes, and checkoffs support the CETa competency matrix.ETA CETa matrix and MCA readiness assessment.

Required Repeated Routine

  1. Check in, inspect PPE, confirm tools, and receive bench assignment.
  2. Complete the daily safety brief.
  3. Review prior learning and connect to the CETa competency target.
  4. Complete new instruction and demonstration.
  5. Perform lab practice with documentation.
  6. Complete practical checkoff or progress check.
  7. Shut down the laboratory and complete AAR.
