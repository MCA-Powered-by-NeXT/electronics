Updated Course Mission

The NeXTHands: Basic Electronics Technician program is an ETA Associate Certified Electronics Technician (CETa) preparation course. Students complete structured safety training, hands-on electronics labs, technician documentation, quizzes, practical checkoffs, and a final readiness assessment aligned to entry-level electronics technician expectations.

Students Should Graduate With

  • MCA Basic Electronics Technician Certificate eligibility.
  • Hands-on technician skills using approved low-voltage classroom training systems.
  • Readiness to sit for the ETA Associate Certified Electronics Technician (CETa) certification exam.

Course Philosophy

The NeXTHands: Basic Electronics Technician program prepares students for entry-level employment by emphasizing safety, professionalism, accountability, documentation, teamwork, and technical competence. Students are expected to perform to industry standards while developing the knowledge and skills necessary to successfully pursue the ETA Associate Certified Electronics Technician (CETa) certification. Safety is not simply a lesson—it is an expectation that applies to every class, every lab, and every task.

Final Mission

Final goal: Graduate safe, professional, entry-level electronics technicians who are prepared to enter the workforce and are ready to sit for the ETA Associate Certified Electronics Technician (CETa) certification exam.

MCA Certification Exam Support Statement

Students who successfully complete the course, complete all labs, pass all required practical checkoffs, meet attendance requirements, and pass the MCA CETa Readiness Assessment may qualify to have their ETA CETa certification exam paid for by MCA, subject to MCA approval, funding availability, and current certification-administrator requirements.
