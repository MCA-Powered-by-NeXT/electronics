Milestone Session Guide

Milestone days verify learning, recover missed work, and prepare students for CETa readiness. No new instruction occurs on milestone days.

DashboardScheduleThree-Hour Class

Milestone Rule

No new instruction on Milestone Days. Milestone sessions are for assessment, coaching, remediation, makeups, readiness practice, and continuous improvement.

Milestone Activities

  • Weekly quizzes
  • Practical exams
  • Practical checkoffs
  • CETa practice questions
  • Troubleshooting practice
  • Lab makeups
  • TestOut or equivalent digital reinforcement
  • Tinkercad reinforcement
  • Final project work
  • Career readiness
  • Instructor coaching
  • Tool inventory
  • Equipment maintenance
  • Weekly After Action Review

Suggested Three-Hour Milestone Block

TimeActivity
10 minStudent check-in, PPE, tool accountability, bench assignment
10 minSafety brief and milestone expectations
30 minWeekly quiz or CETa practice questions
60 minPractical checkoffs, lab makeups, or troubleshooting stations
35 minInstructor coaching, documentation correction, TestOut/Tinkercad reinforcement
20 minTool inventory, equipment maintenance, and lab reset
10 minWeekly AAR and next-week readiness
