Cohort Synchronization

  • Monday day cohort equals Tuesday evening cohort.
  • Wednesday day cohort equals Thursday evening cohort.
  • Friday morning milestone equals the following Monday evening milestone.

Attendance Flexibility

Students may attend the alternate cohort to make up missed instruction when space, instructor approval, and safety requirements allow. Makeup attendance does not remove required performance evidence.

Students must complete the required safety brief, lab, documentation, and checkoffs for the missed instructional block.

Attendance Requirements for MCA CETa Exam Support

  • Attend required class meetings or approved alternate-cohort makeups.
  • Complete all labs and safety brief requirements.
  • Pass all practical checkoffs and the MCA CETa Readiness Assessment.
  • Maintain required documentation and instructor signoffs.
