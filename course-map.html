Standard Class Requirement

Lesson A and Lesson B use the standard three-hour class framework: check-in, safety brief, review, new instruction, instructor demonstration, hands-on lab, practical checkoff, lab shutdown, and AAR.

Milestone Requirement

Milestone sessions contain assessment, checkoffs, practice, makeups, coaching, inventory, maintenance, and AAR. No new instruction occurs on Milestone Days.
