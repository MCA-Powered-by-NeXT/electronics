Battery Safety

Battery handling expectations for classroom electronics labs.

DashboardSafety ManualFire Safety

Core Rules

  • Use only instructor-approved batteries and holders.
  • Check polarity before connecting a battery.
  • Disconnect batteries before changing wiring.
  • Never intentionally short battery terminals.
  • Do not use hot, leaking, corroded, cracked, damaged, or swollen batteries.
  • Report unusual heat, odor, smoke, swelling, or leakage immediately.

Damaged batteriesRemove from student use and notify the instructor.

Lithium awarenessUnderstand that lithium batteries require special caution for heat, swelling, charging, storage, and disposal.
StorageStore batteries in assigned containers away from loose metal objects.
DisposalDispose only through instructor-approved disposal process.

Classroom Response

If a battery becomes hot, damaged, swollen, leaking, or produces odor or smoke, students stop work, move away if needed, notify the instructor, and follow the fire safety and incident response procedure.
