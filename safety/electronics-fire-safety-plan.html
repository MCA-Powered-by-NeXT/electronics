Electronics Fire Safety Plan

Fire prevention and emergency response expectations for low-voltage electronics training.

Fire Prevention

  • Use only approved low-voltage DC training sources.
  • Disconnect power before changing wiring.
  • Do not bypass fuses or protection devices.
  • Keep paper, clutter, and liquids away from active circuits.
  • Report heat, odor, smoke, discoloration, or unusual behavior immediately.

Electrical and Battery Fire Awareness

Electrical and battery fires may begin with heat, odor, smoke, sparking, discoloration, swelling, or abnormal component behavior. Students must disconnect power if safe, stop work, notify the instructor, and follow emergency directions.

Fire Extinguisher Awareness

ClassGeneral Use
AOrdinary combustibles such as paper or wood.
BFlammable liquids.
CEnergized electrical equipment.
DCombustible metals.
KCooking oils and fats.

Students should not attempt firefighting unless trained, the fire is small, the exit is clear, and the instructor or emergency plan permits action.

PASS Method Awareness

  1. Pull the pin.
  2. Aim at the base of the fire.
  3. Squeeze the handle.
  4. Sweep side to side.

Emergency Evacuation and Reporting

  1. Stop work and alert others.
  2. Follow instructor directions.
  3. Evacuate by the posted route if directed.
  4. Report to the assigned assembly area.
  5. Do not re-enter until authorized.
  6. Instructor completes incident documentation.
