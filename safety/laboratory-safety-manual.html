Laboratory Safety Manual

Required safety expectations for the NeXTHands CETa-prep electronics lab.

Safety Boundary

Students use only instructor-approved, low-voltage DC training circuits and approved classroom power sources. Students do not work on wall outlets, building wiring, open power supplies, vehicle electrical systems, charged capacitors, unknown energized equipment, or any non-training device.

Laboratory Rules

  • Wear required PPE before entering active lab work.
  • Keep food, drinks, backpacks, and loose metal objects away from benches.
  • Do not energize, modify, or measure circuits until authorized by the instructor.
  • Disconnect power before wiring changes.
  • Report smoke, odor, heat, damaged leads, damaged tools, swollen batteries, or unclear instructions immediately.
  • Use the correct tool for the task and return tools at shutdown.
  • No student leaves until the instructor verifies shutdown and accountability.

Electrical Safety

  • Assume unknown circuits are unsafe until inspected and verified.
  • Use only approved classroom low-voltage DC training sources.
  • No live AC measurements are performed in this course.
  • Verify meter lead placement and dial position before probing.
  • Never bypass fuses or protection devices.

Battery Safety

  • Check polarity before connecting batteries.
  • Prevent short circuits between battery terminals.
  • Do not use damaged, leaking, hot, or swollen batteries.
  • Store and dispose of batteries according to instructor directions.

Fire Safety

  • Know emergency exits and reporting procedures.
  • Disconnect power when safe before responding to overheating circuits.
  • Notify the instructor immediately for smoke, odor, heat, or visible damage.
  • Use fire extinguishers only if trained and directed by emergency conditions.

Housekeeping

  • Keep benches clean and wiring paths visible.
  • Return components and tools to assigned locations.
  • Remove clipped leads and small debris.
  • Keep aisles and emergency exits clear.

Injury and Incident Reporting

All injuries, near misses, equipment damage, overheating, smoke, odor, or unsafe conditions must be reported to the instructor immediately. The instructor documents the incident and determines whether lab activity can continue.

Student Responsibilities

  • Follow instructions and stop when uncertain.
  • Use PPE and safe procedures every class.
  • Document measurements and corrective actions honestly.
  • Participate in AAR and continuous improvement.

Instructor Responsibilities

  • Prepare safe lab stations and approved equipment.
  • Conduct the daily safety brief.
  • Verify PPE, tools, power state, and lab shutdown.
  • Maintain incident records and AAR logs.
