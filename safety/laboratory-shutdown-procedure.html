Laboratory Shutdown Procedure

Every class ends with verified shutdown, accountability, and instructor approval.

DashboardSafety ManualAAR

Student Shutdown Checklist

  • [ ] Turn off electronics.
  • [ ] Disconnect power.
  • [ ] Remove batteries when required.
  • [ ] Verify circuits are de-energized.
  • [ ] Turn off meters.
  • [ ] Turn off power supplies.
  • [ ] Unplug equipment when directed.
  • [ ] Return tools.
  • [ ] Return components.
  • [ ] Return PPE.
  • [ ] Clean workstation.
  • [ ] Wait for instructor approval before leaving.

Instructor Verification

  • [ ] Equipment is OFF.
  • [ ] Equipment is unplugged where required.
  • [ ] No energized circuits remain.
  • [ ] Tools are accounted for.
  • [ ] Components are returned or documented.
  • [ ] PPE is accounted for.
  • [ ] Lab is clean.
  • [ ] Damage, missing items, or incidents are logged.

Policy

No student leaves the laboratory until the instructor verifies shutdown, tool/PPE accountability, and workstation condition.
