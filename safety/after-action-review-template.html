AAR Purpose

An After Action Review is a short, structured conversation that compares the class mission to what actually happened. It identifies successes, problems, safety concerns, and improvements for the next session.

Discussion Prompts

  • What was today's mission?
  • What were the results?
  • What went well?
  • What problems or confusion appeared?
  • Were there any safety concerns or near misses?
  • What should we improve next time?
  • Were there equipment, tool, PPE, or component issues?
  • What questions remain?
