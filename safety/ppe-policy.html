Policy Statement

All students are required to wear assigned PPE while in the laboratory. Students without required PPE may not participate in lab activity until the PPE issue is corrected and the instructor approves participation.

Minimum PPE

  • ANSI-approved safety glasses.
  • Closed-toe shoes.
  • Additional PPE as assigned by the instructor for specific tasks.

MCA-Provided PPE

  • MCA provides required PPE for student lab participation.
  • PPE remains property of MCA.
  • PPE must remain onsite unless the program director authorizes otherwise.
  • PPE is inspected daily at class check-in.
  • PPE is accounted for at end-of-class shutdown.
