Course Overview

This site is organized as an ETA CETa preparation course for entry-level electronics technician training. The sequence builds from safety and technician fundamentals to DMM use, continuity testing, schematics, circuit building, troubleshooting, documentation, final practical assessment, and MCA CETa readiness review.

Safety boundary: Student labs use assigned low-voltage classroom training circuits only. Students do not work on non-training building power, wall outlets, live AC measurements, open power supplies, vehicle electrical systems, charged capacitors, or unknown energized equipment.
