Final Practical Exam

Capstone hands-on assessment for safe entry-level electronics technician performance.

DashboardReadinessFinal Project Rubric

Exam Purpose

The final practical exam verifies that the student can prepare a safe workstation, identify tools and components, build a simple circuit, use a DMM appropriately, troubleshoot a controlled fault, document results, and shut down the lab correctly.

Practical Stations

StationTaskEvidence
1. Safety and PPEComplete PPE, bench, tool, meter, and power-source inspection.Inspection form and instructor observation.
2. Tool and Component IDIdentify assigned tools, wires, connectors, and components.Instructor questioning and checklist.
3. Breadboard BuildBuild an assigned low-voltage LED/resistor/switch circuit.Working circuit and build notes.
4. DMM MeasurementMeasure approved DC voltage, resistance, or continuity as assigned.Measurement log with units.
5. TroubleshootingDiagnose a simple instructor-created training-board fault.Repair ticket and verification.
6. Shutdown and AARComplete lab shutdown and short reflection.Shutdown checklist and AAR prompt.
