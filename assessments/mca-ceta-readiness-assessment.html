MCA CETa Readiness Assessment

Internal readiness screen before a student is recommended to sit for the ETA CETa exam.

DashboardCETa MatrixFinal Practical

Purpose

The MCA CETa Readiness Assessment verifies whether a student has completed the course requirements, demonstrated safe hands-on performance, and shown sufficient knowledge readiness for the ETA Associate Certified Electronics Technician exam.

Eligibility Checklist

  • [ ] Student successfully completed the course.
  • [ ] Student completed all required labs.
  • [ ] Student passed all practical checkoffs.
  • [ ] Student met attendance or approved makeup requirements.
  • [ ] Student completed final project documentation.
  • [ ] Student passed the final practical exam.
