Week 1 CETa Alignment

Primary focus: safety precautions, PPE, hazard recognition, technician habits, ESD/LOTO awareness, low-voltage training boundaries, and basic circuit thinking.

Required Three-Hour Routine

Lesson A and Lesson B must follow the standard three-hour class framework: check-in, safety brief, review, new instruction, instructor demonstration, hands-on lab, practical checkoff, shutdown, and AAR.
